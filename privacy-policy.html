Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

The Short Version

PingMeter does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not store your speed test results on any server. The test runs in your browser. However, like every real website, we rely on infrastructure and advertising partners that process limited technical data — this policy explains exactly what happens, with no contradictions and no hidden tracking.

What We Store on Your Device

  • Test history: Your last 10 results (date, download, upload, ping, jitter) are saved in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. They never leave your phone or computer.
  • Cookie choice: Whether you accepted or declined cookies is also stored locally.
  • How to delete: Clear your browser's site data, or tap "Clear" in the Recent Tests panel on the home page.

What Happens During a Speed Test

To measure your connection, your browser transfers data with Cloudflare's speed test endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com). During this transfer, Cloudflare — our hosting and measurement provider — necessarily sees technical connection data such as your IP address, in order to deliver the data. Cloudflare processes this data under its own privacy policy as part of operating its network. We do not receive, log, or store your IP address or your test results on any server we control.

Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This means:

  • Google may associate ad interactions with a cookie identifier — not with your name or email.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • Visitors in the EEA and UK are shown a consent message before ad cookies are set. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are served.
  • For full details on how Google uses data when you visit partner sites, see: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell personal information. We do not run third-party analytics trackers on this site. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, we cannot access, export, or delete personal records — none exist. Data stored on your device is fully under your control (see "What We Store on Your Device"). For requests relating to data processed by Cloudflare or Google, those providers offer their own privacy controls and request processes. You may also contact us using the details below and we will assist where we can.

Children

PingMeter is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a partner service, contact us and we will act promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will always reflect the latest revision. Material changes will be indicated by a updated effective date on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via our Contact page.
